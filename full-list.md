# EA and AI-safety organisations on the UK sponsor register

Source: Home Office register of licensed sponsors, downloaded 2026-11-09 ([register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers)).
Every match below is A-rated (Google is "A (Premium)"). No B-rated matches.
Legal names were checked against Companies House and the Charity Commission (officers or former names) where the register name differs from the common name.

Routes: SW = Skilled Worker; GBM = Global Business Mobility: Senior or Specialist Worker (intra-company transfers only, not usable for a new hire from outside); CW = Temporary Worker: Charity Worker (unpaid voluntary work only).
Size is a rough guess: S under 15 UK staff, M 15–60, L over 60, ? unknown.
"Services" says whether the org sells or provides services to other orgs, which matters if the host should let the consultant do AI-uplift work for other EA orgs.
