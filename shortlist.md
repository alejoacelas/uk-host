# Shortlist: UK sponsors for an AI-uplift role

All orgs below are A-rated Skilled Worker sponsors on the Home Office register of 9 Nov 2026. The full list of ~80 licensed EA, AI-safety and adjacent orgs, plus ~110 checked and unlicensed, is in [full-list.md](full-list.md).

The [research](research/) folder has the full list of what each org has hosted, incubated, fiscally sponsored or funded:
- [hubs-ais.md](research/hubs-ais.md): LISA, Arcadia, Meridian, BlueDot.
- [hosts-ea.md](research/hosts-ea.md): Rethink Priorities, Ambitious Impact, Effective Ventures.
- [inhouse.md](research/inhouse.md): 80,000 Hours, Coefficient Giving, Founders Pledge, GovAI, Apollo, Forethought, Faculty, Tomoro.

## What the host has to satisfy

- **Licence:** an A-rated Skilled Worker licence. Global Business Mobility and Charity Worker routes don't cover this hire.
- **Salary:** at least £41,700 and the occupation's going rate. The best-fitting code is 2431 (management consultants and business analysts), with a going rate of £50,200. Build-heavy codes (2133, 2134, 2139) need £52–55k. Under-26 applicants can use 70% of the going rate (£35,100 for 2431).
- **Costs the host pays and can't pass on:** £525 for the certificate of sponsorship, plus the skills charge. For a small or charity sponsor that is £1,440 for three years; for a large one it's £3,960.
- **Contracting out to other orgs** ([sponsor guidance Part 2](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/workers-and-temporary-workers-guidance-for-sponsors-part-2-sponsor-a-worker), S1.34–S1.38): the host must own the outputs of every engagement. Each client engagement must be a project or service with an end date. Placing the worker in an ongoing role at a client is banned even with a contract, and the penalty is losing the licence. Fixed-scope uplift projects (an audit, a build sprint, a coaching cohort) fit. An open-ended embed at one client doesn't.
- **Timing:** the new job starts only after UKVI approves the change-of-sponsor application. The application must be submitted before the curtailed leave runs out.
- **Side work:** freelance self-employment isn't allowed on this visa. Extra employment of up to 20 hours a week in an eligible job is allowed.
