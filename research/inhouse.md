# What each candidate host has sponsored, hosted or funded

Researched 2026-11-09 for the UK visa-sponsor search. "Current" means active in 2026. "None found" means a search turned up nothing; it does not prove absence.

## Summary

- **Coefficient Giving.** Strong internal AI enablement: an AI Enablement Lead since early 2026 and an AI Enablement Manager posting, open until 12 Nov. The Manager role is remote, needs US-hours overlap, and offers no visa sponsorship. CG has no programme funding AI uplift inside grantees; the nearest grant is $40k to HartreeWorks. Of the UK hosts, it funds LISA (about $4.7M) and Arcadia (about $12M) most heavily, and both offer immigration or fiscal-sponsorship services. No CG grants found to Impact Ops, Players Philanthropy Fund (PPF) or Ashgro.
- **80,000 Hours.** Independent of Effective Ventures (EV) since spring 2025, with two UK entities. Its only spin-off was Effective Animal Activism, in 2013, and it hosts no outside projects. It uses AI internally (LLM headhunting, an AI systems specialist on advising), so the realistic route in is a job, not a hosted consultancy.
- **Founders Pledge.** Runs funds: Climate, Global Catastrophic Risks, Catalytic Impact, Patient Philanthropy, Animal Welfare. Its only incubation is the Patient Philanthropy Fund, a trust inside its UK company. Pledge Ventures is its venture arm. No grants for AI adoption found. It has an in-house AI Developer role (London, £65k, probably closed).
- **GovAI.** Runs a large fellowship programme (100+ participants a year, including an Applied Track). It has incubated no orgs, and its UK entity is a company limited by guarantee.
- **Apollo Research.** Was fiscally sponsored by Rethink Priorities (RP) and became a for-profit public benefit corporation (PBC) in Jan 2026. It sells Watcher, an agent-monitoring product. It runs no programmes and hosts no projects.
- **Forethought.** A small UK non-profit company. It lists incubation and fellowships only as things extra funding could pay for.
- **Faculty.** Bought by Accenture (closed Mar 2026). It has done AI safety evaluation for UK AISI (£487k in 2023, about £1.05m in 2024) and red-teamed OpenAI's o1. No nonprofit or EA AI-adoption clients found. CG's grant research agent saw "Faculty AI" in Open Philanthropy's grantee list, but I could not confirm any such grant.
- **Tomoro AI.** OpenAI agreed in May 2026 to buy it for the OpenAI Deployment Company, and tomoro.ai now redirects to deploy.co. Its clients are enterprise (e.g. Virgin Atlantic). No nonprofit or AI-safety clients found.

## Coefficient Giving (formerly Open Philanthropy)

Takeaway: Coefficient Giving (CG) is serious about AI adoption in its own staff. It has an AI Enablement Lead and is hiring an AI Enablement Manager, but that role offers no visa sponsorship. It has no programme that funds AI uplift inside grantee orgs. The UK hosts it funds heavily are LISA and Arcadia.

Method: coefficientgiving.org blocks scripted fetches. Grant data comes from the Trace mirror of the CG database (trace.manifund.org, synced 2026-11-04), cross-checked against aisafety.fund, grantmaking.ai, archived openphilanthropy.org pages and grantee sites. Amounts are in USD.

### AI Enablement Manager (open now, closes 12 Nov 2026, 11:59pm EST)
- [Posting](https://coefficientgiving.org/about-us/careers/ai-enablement-manager/)
- **Team.** Sits in the IT team within Operations, managed by the IT Lead (Luke Willey). It works alongside the AI Enablement Lead (Chris Webster).
- **Goal.** Make CG "the most effective AI user in philanthropy", starting with faster grantmaking and hiring.
- **Work.**
  - Run AI experiments with teams.
  - Build a "paved road" of connectors, permissions and templates.
  - Handle AI security, admin, vendors and spend.
  - Coach staff and run a power-user group.
  - Evaluate always-on agents and prototype grantmaking tools.
- **Pay.** $137k base plus a $20,550 401(k) contribution if remote. It is higher in the SF Bay Area, Washington DC or New York.
- **Location.** Remote. International applicants are OK with at least half-day overlap with US hours.
- **Visa sponsorship.** None: "Unfortunately, we are not able to sponsor visas for this role."

- The 2025 CEO letter frames AI adoption as how CG keeps staffing light. [CEO letter](https://coefficientgiving.org/wp-content/uploads/2025-Letter-from-the-CEO-final.pdf)
