# What four UK AI-safety hubs host, run and incubate

Checked 9 Nov 2026 against each org's live site, Wayback Machine snapshots, EA Forum and LessWrong posts, job ads and the Charity Commission register. "Current" means the org's own site or a 2026 job ad still lists it. "Past" means it appears only in older snapshots or the org says it ended. Where a status couldn't be confirmed, the entry says so.

Relationship labels:

- **Run directly:** the hub's own programme or team.
- **Fiscally sponsored:** a separate project whose money and contracts run through the hub's legal entity.
- **Incubated:** the hub says it started or incubated the project.
- **Office resident:** an organisation working from the hub's space.
- **Hosted programme:** a programme that runs cohorts in the hub's space.

## Which hubs pay staff to serve the orgs they host

| Hub | Staff paid to serve other orgs | Strength of evidence |
|---|---|---|
| LISA | Yes. Its job ads say the orgs it hosts "increasingly ask us for help with the unglamorous work of running themselves well". It is building an "Organisation Support" function and hiring operations and People staff who triage those orgs' requests, deliver legal, HR and IT services, and advise their founders. | Strong: several live 2026 job ads |
| Arcadia Impact | Partly. Its operations team supports its own programmes and its fiscally sponsored projects. One Oct 2026 job ad included **"AI adoption: leading AI adoption in our day-to-day operations by testing tools, building automations for recurring tasks"**. | Strong for internal AI adoption; that ad closed 25 Oct 2026 |
| Meridian | Some. Its finance and operations staff administer fiscal sponsorships for ERA and Geodesic Research, and it says it manages £11M in research grants. Some core-team members hold operations roles inside hosted projects, such as a CAISH Operations Coordinator in 2025. It advertises no service offering to outside orgs. | Medium |
| BlueDot Impact | No evidence. Staff serve course participants, grantees and founders who come through Incubator Week, not organisations. | None found |

### Staff who serve other orgs

The 2026 team is a CEO, a Director of Ecosystem, a **Director of Transformation** (David Arnold), an Operations Lead and two Hub Builders. Other staff listed are a Director of Operations and a Head of Compliance, plus an outside IT consultant.

Live 2026 job ads show LISA building a service function for the orgs it hosts:

- **[Operations Senior Manager](https://www.safeai.org.uk/operations-senior-manager):** "Support the organisations we host … Help to deliver the day-to-day operational services we provide to resident organisations, across legal, HR, and more … develop the service offering … acting as a coordinator between the functional experts and the customers." Covers facilities, HR, IT, legal and programme operations, "both for LISA and for the organisations we support".
- **[Operations Manager](https://www.safeai.org.uk/operations-manager):** "the organisations we host increasingly ask us for help with the unglamorous work of running themselves well"; "the IT systems our team and organisations rely on"; "one of the people our resident organisations turn to with operational problems of their own".
- **[Head of People](https://www.safeai.org.uk/head-of-people):** a People adviser to partner orgs (office hours, workshops, templates, a model handbook). It is advisory "rather than an outsourced HR function". The ad names new 2027 functions: "Talent Mobilisation, Organisation Support, Ecosystem Convening".
- **[Member Experience Associate](https://www.safeai.org.uk/member-experience-associate):** front-of-house services for members.

None of these ads mentions AI tools or AI adoption. The closest fit is the "Organisation Support" function and the unexplained "Director of Transformation" role.

### Staff who serve other orgs

- The operations team is a Business Operations Manager, a People Operations Manager, an Operations Associate and an Operations and Communications Associate. It was recruiting a Director of Operations at £120–144k (deadline 31 Oct 2026).
- The ops team supports Arcadia's programmes and its sponsored projects (Orion, Impact First). The Charity Commission classes Arcadia as "Acts As An Umbrella Or Resource Body". Its charitable objects include "providing free or low cost co-working spaces and other services to charities … aimed at increasing their productivity, efficiency".
- **[Programme Operations Associate, LASR Labs](http://web.archive.org/web/20261002104534/https://www.arcadiaimpact.org/careers/programme-operations-associate)** (£45–50k, closed 25 Oct 2026). Its duties included "AI adoption: leading AI adoption in our day-to-day operations by testing tools, building automations for recurring tasks, and coordinating with the Arcadia team". It is the only ad across the four hubs that names AI adoption as a duty. The job serves Arcadia's own programmes, not outside orgs.

## Gaps

- **Official resident rosters.** LISA, Meridian and Arcadia publish none. The LISA lists above come from homepage logos, testimonials, job ads and partner pages, so smaller residents are probably missing.
- **LISA's Notion membership and strategy pages.** These need JavaScript and couldn't be read.
- **Charity Commission filings.** LISA hasn't filed an annual report yet. Arcadia's first return (FY to Sept 2025) is filed, but I didn't open the accounts PDF. Meridian is a CIC; I didn't pull its Companies House accounts.
- **BlueDot's GitHub repo.** It is no longer public, so the PR that removed the named Incubator Week alumni couldn't be read.
