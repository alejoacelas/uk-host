## 1. Ambitious Impact (UK register: Charity Entrepreneurship, charity 1195850)

- **History:** a project of Charity Science Foundation until it registered in 2021.
- **Size:** £4.33m income and £3.95m spending in the year to March 2025. In Oct 2026 it reported 69 charities launched and $21.5M raised for the next 3–4 years. ([register](https://register-of-charities.charitycommission.gov.uk/en/charity-search/-/charity-details/5173268/financial-history), [Oct 2026 post](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/g4fJLaCmexAxtBpdh/did-i-rejoin-ambitious-impact-at-the-wrong-time))
- **Source of the list:** the visible our-charities page shows only 8 charities, but its raw HTML holds a dataset of 61 with cohort and status. That dataset is the base, checked against each cohort's EA Forum post.
- **Relationship:** "incubated" for every row unless noted. The status column is blank where the dataset says active.

## 2. Effective Ventures (EV UK charity 1149828, company 07962181; EV US EIN 47-1988398)

- **Names:** "Centre for Effective Altruism" until Aug 2022, then Effective Ventures Foundation (UK).
- **Nov 2023:** the boards decided to spin out every project.
- **28 Oct 2024:** EV said it would take no new projects and its entities will "gradually wind down and dissolve".
- **FY25 accounts (filed Feb 2026):** prepared on the basis that EV is closing, with wind-down expected "in 2026 or beyond". Free reserves about £63k.
- **Companies House:** EV UK still registered as of a May 2026 filing. No dissolution of EV UK or EV US confirmed.
- **Sources:** [FTX settlement and future of EV](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/HjsfHwqasyQMWRzZN/ev-updates-ftx-settlement-and-the-future-of-ev), EV FY24 and FY25 accounts, archived ev.org/organisations pages 2022–2026.

## 3. Rethink Priorities (RP US 501(c)(3) EIN 84-3896318; Rethink Priorities UK Ltd 14807023)
